IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA

CIVIL / CRIMINAL APPELLATE JURISDICTION

Special Leave Petition (Civil) No. 4920 of 2024

I. The Matter

This petition concerns the interpretation of fundamental rights under Part III of the Constitution, specifically the interplay between procedural fairness and evidentiary standards in high-stakes technological crimes. The Petitioner seeks a writ of certiorari against the high court's previous determination.

II. Issues Framed

  1. Whether the integrity of hash-values in digital forensic logs constitutes a 'primary fact' under the Indian Evidence Act.
  2. The scope of judicial review in matters involving automated algorithmic sentencing recommendations.

III. Judicial Reasoning

Upon careful examination of the forensic logs presented in the Annexure series 'D', this bench finds a significant deviation in the chain of custody. The doctrine of *stare decisis* compels us to look at the precedent set in *State of Punjab v. Modern Electronics (2019)*, wherein it was established that digital authenticity cannot be presumed in the absence of a valid Section 65B certificate.
